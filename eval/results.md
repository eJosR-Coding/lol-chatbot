## Findings

Written by hand from the run above; it is preserved when `eval/run_eval.py` regenerates this file.

Retrieval:

- Top-1 retrieval on paraphrased in-KB questions is weak: 6 of 15 (40%). A verbatim KB question always returns itself (239/239), so the gap comes from paraphrase, not from the index.
- Wrong top-1 hits are not low-confidence. "¿Qué es un campeón tanque?" retrieved "¿Qué es un campeón manaless?" at cosine 0.965, above every correct hit in the set. Entry #139 (manaless) acts as a hub for any question containing "campeón": it was the top-1 for 8 of the 30 queries, including unrelated ones. The embeddings cover only the KB question text (not the answer), and short template questions ("¿Qué es un campeón X?") are matched on shape rather than on the rare term.
- The English glossary terms that users paraphrase or translate (tank/tanque, stun/aturdimiento, ult/habilidad definitiva, KS/robar una kill) are the main misses. In "¿Qué significa que te hicieron un gank?" the exact entry "¿Qué es un gank?" ranked third (0.652) behind "care o cuídate" (0.727).
- The 0.45 threshold separates unrelated questions well (7 of 7 out-of-domain rejected) but cannot detect wrong in-domain hits, because those score 0.56 to 0.97. It also passes 3 of 8 League questions that the KB does not cover (Ahri's passive 0.668, number of champions 0.606, world champion 0.565).
- In the sweep, raising the threshold from 0.30 to 0.60 never changes (a) accuracy: no correct hit scores below 0.60, but 0.30 to 0.40 lets most unrelated questions through. 0.45 is the lowest tested value that rejects all 7 out-of-domain questions. Only 0.60 rejects 6 of 8 uncovered questions, at no tested cost on (a).
- Threshold behaviour in the app: only scores below 0.30 on the first turn produce the "No entendí esa" refusal. Of the 15 questions in (b) and (c), 12 score below 0.45 but only 1 ("¿Cómo instalo Python en Windows?", 0.247) would be refused outright. The other 11 reach the LLM without KB context.

End-to-end (5 samples, one run, `qwen2.5:0.5b`, qualitative):

- When retrieval is right, the rewrite stays mostly faithful but adds noise: the Baron answer keeps the KB facts (strongest neutral monster, buffs, AD/AP, minute 20) yet invents "franquicia ... juegos de cuarto turno (T4)" and says "se lanza"; the burst answer restates the KB sentence and then garbles the last line ("los otros comandantes").
- When retrieval is wrong, the model follows the wrong context or improvises: the Flash question was answered from the "summoner" entry with an incorrect explanation.
- Without context the model answers anyway and is not grounded: it states "Ahri no tiene ninguna habilidad pasiva" (false) and gives a made-up cooking method for the tortilla de papas (score 0.328, context dropped, but not refused).
- Conclusion: the pipeline is faithful to the retrieved entry only about as often as retrieval is correct, and a 0.5B model does not reliably stay on topic once the context is dropped.

Caveats: 30 hand-written questions is a small sample, so percentages move by 3 to 7 points per question; the question set was fixed before the first run and was not edited afterwards; the (a) paraphrases range from near-verbatim to loose (for example "rotísimo" for "broken"), so 40% is not an estimate of real-user accuracy. Conversation history (which can raise follow-up scores in the app) is not exercised.
